## Screens and navigation (all need capture confirmation)

- **Opening the phone:** tap D-pad Up. Hold D-pad Up to go straight to WAiNK. (GB, BB: accepted.)
- **Calls** are made only through Contacts; there is no dialer. (GB. NDB agrees that contacts have a Call action: accepted.)
- **Contacts:** an A–Z list with letter tabs and a "Suggested" list. Each contact has Call, Open WhatUp and Open Snapmatic actions. (NDB only: unverified.)
- **WhatUp:** conversation threads. Messages can carry photos or coordinates that drop map markers. (GB, NDB: accepted.) NDB also says threads are marked encrypted and replies are preset. *Unverified.*
- **Camera:** take photo, light, grid, selfie, zoom, filter, gallery shortcut and exit. (NDB lists these as controls. BB lists selfie, poses, expressions, filters, zoom, flashlight and grid. The overlapping controls are accepted.)
- **Gallery:** camera photos plus story photos (BB). Storage, options, select and multi-delete menus (NDB). *Partly unverified.*
- **Snapmatic:** a browse-only feed with follow, like, comment and creator profiles. You cannot post. (BB, GB, NDB: accepted.)
- **Settings:** wallpaper, ringtone, and third-party plugins such as a cop scanner and a key cloner. (BB, NDB: accepted.)
- **BuckMe:** checking account and a shared Family Savings account (GB, NDB: accepted). ATM finder (GB only). Savings goal (NDB only).
- **RydeMe:** choose a destination and see the fare before booking. (GB only: unverified.)
- **Scooter Bros:** request a car from the garage, set the destination, rename the car. (NDB: unverified.)
- **Fitness:** Strength, Cardio and Focus levels (NDB). Strength, cardio, focus, nutrition, calories and body condition (BB). The overlap is accepted: Strength, Cardio, Focus.
